# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, design leaders, founders, product partners, and recruiters evaluating Kousik Dutta for senior product design roles. They arrive with limited time, scan for judgment first, then look for proof that the work is clear, shipped, collaborative, and buildable.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Kousik as a senior, AI-first product designer who turns complex systems into calm, useful products. Success means the site earns trust quickly, makes the strongest work easy to understand, and helps the right viewer feel confident enough to start a hiring conversation.

## Brand Personality

Smooth, sharp, collaborative. The voice should feel calm, precise, human, and quietly confident, as if Kousik is speaking directly to the viewer without overselling.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic agency templates, decorative glassmorphism everywhere, repeated all-caps section scaffolding, noisy case-study decks, crowded proof blocks, gradient text, and portfolio pages that feel like screenshots dumped into cards. The work should not feel like an AI-generated landing page, a Framer clone, or a résumé pasted into a website.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with thinking, then show craft as proof.
2. Make every section earn its space and remove anything that does not help Kousik get hired.
3. Use liquid glass only where it improves orientation, affordance, or focus.
4. Keep copy simple, first-person, benefit-led, and easy to scan.
5. Show real product evidence in polished containers, never raw presentation slides.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, semantic structure, keyboard-visible focus, descriptive alt text, reduced-motion support, native text cursor behavior, and clear light plus dark mode rendering.
