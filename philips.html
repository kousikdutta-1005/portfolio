CONTEXT

Immersed in Philips' design culture, studied peers, synthesized insights into a clear problem statement.

What is CAD?

CAD is artery narrowing that reduces heart blood flow, causing angina and heart attacks.

Symptoms

Symptoms include angina, breathlessness, fatigue, palpitations, dizziness, nausea, and heart attack.

Risk factors

Risks include smoking, high BP, high LDL, diabetes, obesity, unhealthy diet, age, family history, & male sex.

Problem Statement

How might we create a comprehensive solution to reduce the risk factors linked to CAD among Indian adults, considering its potential for heart attacks and cardiac arrests?

RESEARCH

Defined research questions, reviewed literature, analyzed competitors, identified gaps, & crafted user interview questions.

Literature Review: Insights

6 categories from 141 sources

CAD Risk Factors

A sedentary lifestyle, poor diet, metabolic conditions, and inequities elevate CAD risk in Indians 30+.

Stress Management

Managing chronic stress and mental health reduces behaviors and physiologic drivers linked to CAD.

Culture & Society

Promote healthy diets, address socioeconomic gaps, and lower cultural barriers to regular activity.

Tech Interventions

Digital self‑monitoring and nudges can support behavior change and targeted prevention at scale.

Environmental Factors

Pollution, limited green spaces, and urban design influence cardiovascular risk profiles.

Device Risk Classification

A classification helps providers select appropriate monitors to manage individual risk profiles.

ANALYSIS

Transcribed interviews in Excel, built an inductive codebook, distilled four themes, and produced user stories, stakeholder maps, and a persona revealing CAD prevention opportunities.

"As is" User Story

Phase 1/4: College Life (18-23Y)

Poor diet, long sitting, late nights, smoking or alcohol, and ignored self care dominate.

Phase 2/4: Starts professional life (24-27Y)

Work disrupts meals, extends sitting, adds parties, increases alcohol or smoking, and neglects self care.

Phase 3/4: Marries and starts a family (28-32Y)

Diet improves slightly, but sitting persists, stress rises, weight increases, BP and cholesterol emerge, self care slips.

Phase 4/4: CAD symptoms starts (35+Y)

Better diet and walks start, less alcohol or smoking, chest pain leads to tests and treatment.

DESIGN

Holistic Heart Health Management Ecosystem

Ecosystem

This page of the central web app provides the user with the overview of their whole ecosystem which includes all connected devices, apps, & provides bit sized insights.

Learn

The learn sections hosts educational content about coronary artery disease in form of short videos, articles & blogs from legit resources.

Routine

The routine section gives the user tailored lifestyle modification suggestions integrated into their existing routines for a heart-heathier lifestyle.

Track

Users can track their health across multiple connected apps and services in one place, with personalized insights and lifestyle tips provided for each metric to maximize benefits.

Health Wiz

Health Wiz, an AI health companion, simplifies medical jargon, explains health activities, & offers various helpful features for easy understanding & management of health-related matters.

Lifestyle Change Suggestions

WhatsApp prompt from "Health Wiz" notes user's health changes. User inquires, and Health Wiz directs them to central web app for analysis and suggests lifestyle modifications based on data.
