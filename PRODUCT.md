# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, design leaders, recruiters, and product teams evaluating Kousik Dutta for senior product design roles. They arrive with limited time, often comparing many portfolios, and need to quickly understand how Kousik thinks, how he shapes systems, how he crafts interfaces, and how he can use AI-assisted building to make ideas real.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio exists to make Kousik's judgment visible. It should sell his thinking first, his craft second, and his ability to prototype or build with AI-assisted code third. Success means a senior design evaluator leaves with a clear sense of Kousik's taste, product judgment, systems thinking, visual craft, and readiness for hard product work.

## Brand Personality

Calm, sharp, and personal. The voice should feel direct and confident, like Kousik speaking clearly to another designer or product leader. The interface should feel smooth, precise, quietly premium, and unmistakably made by someone with taste rather than by a template.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-generated portfolio scaffolds, repetitive card grids, decorative glassmorphism, nested surfaces, ornamental glows, tiny uppercase section labels repeated everywhere, filler icons, trophy-style proof, excessive whitespace, crowded whitespace, and copy that sounds like a template. Do not let the site feel like a SaaS landing page, a Dribbble shot, or a design-system sample page. Apple-like restraint is welcome, but only when it makes the story clearer.

## Design Principles

1. Kousik is the hero, not the UI decoration.
2. Thought first, craft second, AI-assisted building third.
3. One surface, one job: use glass, cards, motion, icons, and labels only when they clarify structure or interaction.
4. Keep every page compact, balanced, legible, and calm while preserving enough breathing room for hierarchy.
5. Light and dark modes must both feel intentionally designed, with contrast, material, and depth tuned per mode.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target accessible, legible portfolio browsing for evaluators on desktop and mobile. Maintain strong text contrast, keyboard focus visibility, semantic landmarks, screen-reader-safe state labels, reduced-motion alternatives, and color choices that do not depend on color alone to communicate meaning.
